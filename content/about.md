I am Jiahao Xuan (玄甲豪), a PhD student at Monash University.

I started my PhD in 2025, supervised by Amin Sakzad, Ron Steinfeld, and Muhammad Esgin. My research focuses on lattice-based cryptography and post-quantum cryptographic foundations.

This website collects my research interests, publications, academic notes, and CV updates.

You can also find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/Swavaid) and [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=LlPpVCMAAAAJ&hl=en).
